13,000 years ago, someone forgot a bowl of soaked grain in a cave… and a week later, they got drunk.

But humans didn't really invent alcohol. We inherited a taste for it from apes, ten million years ago. In this video: the drunken monkey hypothesis, the oldest beer ever found, Göbekli Tepe's giant stone tubs, 9,000-year-old Chinese rice wine, Sumerian golden straws and beer wages, the Egyptian goddess who got so drunk she saved humanity, spit beer, 400 drunk Aztec rabbits, ancient beer pong — and the wild idea that beer may have helped build civilization.
